### Privacy Policy for Gotouge Bot
Effective Date: April 26, 2025
Your privacy is important to us. Here's what you should know:
- Data Collection
Gotouge Bot does not collect, store, or share any personal user data. It simply sends preloaded quotes and does not process, track, or log user interactions beyond what Discord natively provides.
- Third-Party Services
Gotouge Bot relies on Discord’s platform to function. Use of Discord itself is subject to Discord's Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.
- Security
While no internet service can be 100% secure, we take reasonable measures to ensure the Bot operates safely.
- Children’s Privacy
Gotouge Bot is intended for general audiences. However, users under the age of 13 must have permission from a parent or guardian to use Discord as per Discord’s policies.
- Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we do, we will post a notice through the Bot’s support page or repository.
